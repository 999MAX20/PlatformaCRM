1 Purpose
This notice describes browser storage on the Platforma CRM information landing page and disclosure of technologies in the working CRM. Platforma Digital LLP, BIN 260940027670, is responsible for its own processing; contact office@platformadigital.kz. This notice accompanies the policy and does not replace consent where required.
2 Confirmed landing page technology
Name: platforma-crm-language. Type: localStorage. Provider: Platforma CRM website code. Value: selected language ru, kk or en. Purpose: retain the language preference between visits. Category: functional preference. The code specifies no expiry: it remains until changed, cleared or removed by the browser. Controls: language selector and browser site-storage settings. localStorage itself does not send the value to a third party; website and hosting network behaviour must be assessed separately.
The inspected local landing-page code contains no embedded third-party analytics, advertising pixels, own enquiry form or payment form. This finding concerns the inspected build, not all hosting technologies or future versions. It does not mean that no cookies exist at all.

4 Controls and changes
Users may change language or clear site data in their browser. This may reset preferences and, in the working CRM, end a session. Essential security mechanisms must not be disabled in ways that make access unsafe; explain their necessity and scope.
Before adding analytics or advertising, assess the basis and transfers. Where consent is required, do not activate the technology before a separate voluntary choice; provide clear refusal and withdrawal. Continued browsing is not universal consent. Determine banner requirements from actual technologies and Kazakhstan law rather than automatically applying a foreign template.
