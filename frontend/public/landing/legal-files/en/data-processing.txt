2 Record and instructions
Before real data transfer, the record specifies subjects, exact fields, sources, purposes and bases, consent evidence, operations, systems and recipients, locations of databases, files, logs and backups, remote access countries, retention, deletion, contacts and safeguards. Record: {{PROCESSING_PASSPORT}}, version {{PASSPORT_VERSION}}. Medical records, diagnoses, scans and biometrics are excluded by default.
Only {{AUTHORIZED_CLINIC_CONTACTS}} issue instructions through {{INSTRUCTION_CHANNEL}}, preserving their history. Platforma checks authority and flags instructions outside scope or apparently unlawful; the affected operation is suspended pending resolution. Legally mandatory action is documented separately, notifying the Clinic where permitted.
3 Lawful disclosure and medical confidentiality
The Clinic establishes lawful collection and disclosure, informs individuals and preserves evidence. On justified request it supplies necessary confirmation without excessive identity documents. Platforma does not accept knowingly unlawful datasets or treat the contract as a substitute for consent.
The parties separately assess Article 273 of the Health Code: seeking medical assistance itself is protected. Patient data require a confirmed lawful route and access restrictions. Without confirmation, use synthetic demonstration data. New medical functions require a separate product change, not expansion of an unrestricted comment field.
4 Personnel and safeguards
Personnel access is limited by role, task and duration. Individual accounts, authority checks, confidentiality and revocation on role change are required. Support accesses content only for a registered ticket or documented incident necessity, with minimum scope and logged actions.
Before launch, Platforma evidences controls appropriate to its processing category: secure channels, roles, authentication, logging, backups, updates, malware protection and response. Applicable categories require multifactor authentication and other mandatory controls. Certification and specific performance metrics must not be claimed without evidence. Implemented-control matrix: {{SECURITY_CONTROL_MATRIX}}.
5 Suppliers and cross-border flows
Only recipients in {{RECIPIENT_REGISTER}} are permitted, specifying role, country, fields, purpose, retention and basis. Platforma obtains necessary contractual safeguards, limits purposes and monitors compliance. Suppliers receive no broader rights than Platforma lawfully holds.
Recipient or country changes require notice {{SUBPROCESSOR_NOTICE_PERIOD}} before transfer. The Clinic may raise justified objections; the affected transfer does not start until resolution. The parties agree an alternative, disable the function or terminate the affected portion with settlement. Obtain renewed individual consent where required.
Check localisation under Article 12 of the Personal Data Law and foreign processing and access under Article 16 and applicable restrictions. Cross-border consent does not remove Kazakhstan storage requirements. An unknown provider, country or retention setting means the channel is not authorised for real data.
6 Requests and incidents
Platforma logs individual requests, applies proportionate identity checks and forwards them to the authorised Clinic contact without undue delay. Internal target {{REQUEST_FORWARD_TARGET}} must permit the shortest statutory deadline. Platforma assists with search, correction, blocking and deletion within its systems and duties.
On an incident, Platforma contains the threat, preserves evidence and immediately informs the Clinic through {{INCIDENT_CONTACTS}}. Contractual maximum {{INCIDENT_NOTICE_MAX}} must not jeopardise statutory notification. Under the verified Article 25, the obligated person notifies the competent authority of a data-security breach within one working day of discovery. Initial notification must not wait for a completed investigation; each party fulfils its applicable duties independently.
The notice includes known timing and nature, systems, categories and volume, possible effects, measures, contacts and next update. Missing information follows. Notices to individuals, authorities and public statements are coordinated without restricting legal deadlines or powers.
7 Compliance checks
Platforma provides reasonably necessary compliance information, check results and deletion evidence. Clinic verification follows {{AUDIT_PROCEDURE}} and secure agreed scope, without exposing other customers' data, secrets or live keys. Reviewers must maintain confidentiality. Confirmed deficiencies are addressed through an owned, dated plan; contractual checks do not restrict state supervision.
8 Exit
Provide the contractual secure export on termination. Delete active data by {{ACTIVE_DELETE_DEADLINE}} and backups by {{BACKUP_EXPIRY}}, issuing corresponding supplier instructions. The certificate records systems, categories, dates, method, executor, verification and lawfully retained exceptions. Expiring backups remain outside ordinary use; restoration reapplies the deletion register.
Confidentiality and safeguards remain while a party can access data. Liability follows the agreement and law; subcontracting does not remove Platforma's own duties.
