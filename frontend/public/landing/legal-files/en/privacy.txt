1 Scope and participants
Platforma Digital LLP, business identification number (BIN) 260940027670, registered address: 77, 140 Prokofyev Street, Almaly District, Almaty, 050006, Republic of Kazakhstan (Platforma), explains the processing of data relating to website visitors, clinic representatives and Platforma CRM users. Data enquiries: office@platformadigital.kz; general enquiries: info@platformadigital.kz; telephone: +7 706 446 82 41.
Platforma determines its own purposes for enquiries, contractual contacts and service security. Each clinic determines the purposes for its patient appointments, correspondence and staff operations. Platforma processes those data within the clinic's instructions and its own statutory duties. “Owner”, “operator” and “third party” have the meanings established by Kazakhstan law; their functions are recorded in the data processing agreement. This allocation does not remove either participant's statutory obligations.
The CRM supports enquiries, contacts, appointments, schedules, tasks and staff. It is not intended for medical records, diagnoses, treatment prescriptions or clinical decisions. Appointment information may itself reveal that a person sought medical assistance and attract medical confidentiality protection.
2 Data categories and purposes
Clinic representatives and prospective customers: full name, position, organisation, business telephone or email, selected channel, enquiry content, negotiations and contract details. Purposes: responding, demonstrations, preparing and performing a contract. Source: the representative or an authorised organisation. Basis: evidenced consent or another specifically applicable lawful basis. A contract with a legal entity does not by itself replace the basis for processing an individual's data.
Clinic users: name, business contacts, role, branch, account settings, actions and security events. Purposes: access provision, permissions, contract performance and service protection. The clinic ensures a lawful basis for providing staff data and informs its staff.
Patients and representatives: name and agreed identifier, contact, appointment and status, practitioner, branch, organisational correspondence, consent and representative authority information. The precise fields are set by the clinic in the contractual schedule. Purposes: handling enquiries, organising visits, changing appointments and service notifications. The clinic records the basis. Diagnoses, scans, individual identification numbers, identity document copies and payment credentials are outside the standard CRM data list.
Technical information: session, device, IP and event information only as actually approved in {{TECH_FIELDS}}. Purposes: operation, abuse prevention and error investigation. Logs are not used for advertising without a separate basis.
3 Operations and minimisation
Within the stated purpose, operations may include collection, accumulation, storage, use, amendment, disclosure to permitted persons, blocking, depersonalisation, anonymisation, deletion and destruction. Access is based on role and necessity. Support uses the minimum necessary extract; an entire database must not be supplied merely for convenience. Production data must not appear in public demonstrations, public links or test environments without an appropriate basis and safeguards.
4 Recipients and locations
Before processing starts, complete and publish {{RECIPIENT_REGISTER}}, specifying each recipient's name, function, data categories, processing and remote access countries, retention and transfer basis. Identify hosting, backups, email, support, messaging and AI separately. Platforma personnel access is restricted to duties and confidentiality commitments.
Storage must comply with localisation requirements under Article 12 of Kazakhstan's Personal Data Law. Actual locations of the main database, files, logs and backups: {{HOSTING_MAP}}. This draft does not certify existing infrastructure compliance. Cross-border transfers require compliance with Article 16 and other applicable requirements; routes, recipients and bases must be disclosed before transfer. Choosing WhatsApp or another channel is not blanket consent to all subsequent transfers.
Following an external link opens a service whose provider's terms govern that provider's own processing. Transfers made by Platforma or a clinic through an integration remain subject to their own duties, consent requirements and contract.
5 Retention and deletion
Enquiry retention relates to responding and completing negotiations; user retention to authority and evidence of actions; patient retention to clinic purposes and applicable duties; log retention to demonstrated security needs. Exact periods, start events, exceptions and backup expiry must be set in {{RETENTION_SCHEDULE}} before launch. Indefinite retention for possible future use is not permitted.
Data are deleted or destroyed once the purpose is achieved unless a lawful retention basis remains. Mandatory retention or disputes justify retaining only the necessary portion under restricted access. Restoring a backup must reapply completed deletions. Contract termination does not mean all backups vanish immediately; the actual expiry cycle must be disclosed.
6 Rights and enquiries
Individuals may obtain legally required information and access, request correction, blocking or deletion, and withdraw consent. Contact office@platformadigital.kz with contact details, the request and, where relevant, the clinic. Identity checks must be proportionate; identity document copies are not requested automatically.
The clinic decides patient-data matters within its competence. Platforma logs requests, securely forwards them to an authorised clinic contact and assists without disregarding its own duties. Consent withdrawal results in cessation or a reasoned refusal within the statutory period: fifteen working days under the verified version of Article 8. Article 25 specifies shorter periods for certain actions, including one working day; the general withdrawal period does not replace these.
Withdrawing optional marketing consent does not prevent core service use. If a particular appointment or function cannot be provided without necessary data, this is explained separately. Individuals may approach the competent authority or court through the applicable procedure.
7 AI and automation
AI is enabled only for an agreed scenario. Users are told that AI is involved, its purpose and how to reach a person. Diagnosis and treatment prescriptions are excluded. Transmitted fields, provider, countries, retention and training settings are documented in the AI schedule. General CRM consent does not authorise training models on clinic data; a separate lawful basis and agreement are required.
8 Safeguards and changes
Platforma establishes safeguards appropriate to its processing category: access control, authentication, logging, secure channels, backups and incident response. Implemented measures must be evidenced before operation. This policy does not promise absolute security.
