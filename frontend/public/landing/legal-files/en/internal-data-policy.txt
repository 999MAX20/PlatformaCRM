2 Processing register and authorisation
Before each process starts, record owner, purpose, statutory role, individuals, fields, source, basis, consent and version, systems, recipients, storage and access countries, retention, deletion, risks, controls, responsible person and approval date. New purposes require a new assessment.
Initial list: clinic representatives — name, business contacts, position and correspondence; users — name, contact, role, branch and access events; patients — minimal appointment and administrative communication data under clinic instructions; representatives — minimum authority information; technical logs — approved fields only. Individual identification numbers, identity copies, biometrics, medical records and diagnoses require separate justification and legal assessment before addition.
Classify the operator and third party using unique individuals across all systems and the category increase for restricted-access data. Small-business status does not substitute for this classification. The verified Rules No. 179/НҚ, edition of 3 September 2026, increase the category by one level for restricted-access data. The responsible person and legal reviewer record classification and Article 10-1 notification applicability before processing and revisit them on growth or scope changes.
3 Retention and bases
For every category, record purpose, definite or determinable period, start event, legal justification, active systems, backups, executor and deletion method. Separate entries cover leads, contracts and accounting, staff accounts, patient appointments, correspondence, consents, individual requests, security logs, backups, marketing suppression and AI logs.
A blank period does not authorise indefinite retention. Dispute holds require a reasoned decision, restricted scope and review. Do not retain data from one purpose for a possible new purpose without a basis. Replace production data in development and demonstrations with synthetic or irreversibly anonymised data.
4 Access and confidentiality
Access request fields: person, role, system, clinic or environment, justification, approver, start, expiry, authentication and logging. Grant least privilege after approval and confidentiality confirmation. Separate administrative from ordinary rights; shared accounts are prohibited.
At employment or contract end or role change, revoke access when authority ends, block sessions, keys and integration tokens and check devices and copies. Review permissions under {{ACCESS_REVIEW_SCHEDULE}}. Apply category-specific mandatory controls for restricted data, including multifactor authentication where required. Missing implementation is a prerequisite task, not a reason to omit a requirement.
Staff or supplier undertaking: “I use entrusted data only within my task and authority, do not disclose it to outsiders or copy it to personal clouds or AI services, follow safeguards, report incidents immediately, and return or delete copies when access ends. Confidentiality survives work completion for the applicable legal protection period.” Name, organisation, role, date and signature: {{CONFIDENTIALITY_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT}}.
5 Consents and individual requests
Before consent-based collection, supply the complete text, purpose, data, recipients, countries and period. Obtain an evidenced affirmative act and separately check representatives. Record person, verification, text version and hash, language, timestamp and timezone, channel, action, recipients, withdrawal, executor and result. New wording must not rewrite old events.
For a request: record receipt time; identify subject matter and applicable deadline; perform proportionate identity checks; determine Platforma and Clinic competence; restrict disputed processing where required; implement, notify and retain evidence. Do not apply one deadline to all requests. Verified Article 8 provides fifteen working days for withdrawal cessation or a reasoned refusal; Article 25 provides certain one-working-day actions. A legal reviewer approves {{LEGAL_REQUEST_DEADLINES}} for all applicable requests.
Request form: number, individual, channel, date, issue, identity check, systems, responsible person, statutory deadline, actions and dates, retention or refusal basis, reply and verification. Do not send patient exports to an unverified address. Forward clinic-related requests without undue delay while fulfilling Platforma's own duties.
6 Incidents
Log every suspicion immediately through {{INCIDENT_INTERNAL_CHANNEL}}. The responder limits access and propagation, preserves logs without altering evidence and involves the responsible person and management. Do not destroy evidence for faster restoration or disclose incident contents in general chats.
Incident form: discovery and suspected start times, source, systems and clinics, subject and data categories, estimated volume, nature, effects, containment, evidence, owners, notices, updates and corrective actions. Initial reports may identify unknowns with a follow-up plan.
Assess duties to notify clinics, individuals and the authority. Where Article 25 applies, notify the authority within one working day of discovering a security breach. Do not wait for a completed investigation or client permission when independent notification is required. The contractual clinic deadline must allow statutory compliance. After remediation, verify recurrence prevention and approve corrective measures.
7 Backup and restoration
{{BACKUP_PLAN}} defines systems, Kazakhstan location with review of other routes, encryption and keys, frequency, backup retention, separate access, success checks, recovery point objective (RPO) and recovery time objective (RTO). Approve values after testing. Test restoration under {{RESTORE_TEST_SCHEDULE}} and record date, copy, scope, outcome, elapsed time, errors and remediation.
Backups are not indefinite alternative archives. Restoration reapplies deletions, revoked access and current restrictions. Do not place personal data in staff personal clouds; every new recipient requires register and legal review.
8 Export and deletion
Export follows a verified authorised request and clinic/scope checks. Before delivery, verify format, completeness, exclusion of other customers' data and secure channel. Deliver passwords or keys separately. Remove temporary exports within the approved period.
Deletion certificate: basis, categories, systems and recipients, deadline, method, executor, independent verification, backups and expiry, mandatory retention exceptions. Removing a UI record is not proof of deletion everywhere. Obtain supplier confirmation. Restrict expiring backups; restoration must not return deleted data to ordinary use.
9 Technical and organisational controls
Before authorisation, compare actual architecture with applicable Rules No. 179/НҚ, including increased category, cryptographic protection and additional controls. HTTPS alone is not proof of all requirements. The control matrix records requirement, applicability, implementation, owner, evidence and gap.
Required processes include system and supplier inventory, safe updates, device protection, vulnerability, secret and access management, clinic separation, significant-action logging, export control, training and knowledge checks. Schedule and evidence: {{SECURITY_AND_TRAINING_PLAN}}. Review materials must not contain real patient data unnecessarily.
