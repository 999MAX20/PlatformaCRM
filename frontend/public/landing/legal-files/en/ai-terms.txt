AI and external integration terms
DRAFT FOR REVIEW
Version 0.1 · 16 October 2026
This document is not operative until approved and the marked fields are completed. Changes require a new version, an effective date and any necessary notice or agreement. Russian is proposed as the prevailing text to the extent permitted by law.

1 Activation
Schedule to agreement {{CONTRACT_NUMBER}} with {{CLINIC_LEGAL_NAME}}. Enable AI and external channels only after approval of {{AI_SCENARIO_CARD}}. Unused functions remain disabled. The card identifies provider and product, purpose, scenario owner, permitted actions, data, countries, retention, supplier access, training settings, cost, restrictions and shutdown method.
A presentation feature does not prove safe readiness. Before real data, test synthetic cases and check permissions, routes and bases. Determine AI classification and applicable duties from actual purpose; the label “assistant” does not itself exclude elevated risk.
2 Permitted scenario and human support
The agreed administrative scenario may answer from approved clinic information, collect a minimal booking request, suggest available times and transfer to an administrator. AI does not diagnose, prescribe, interpret scans, assess medical urgency or determine eligibility for treatment.
Before interaction display: “This is clinic {{CLINIC_NAME}}'s AI appointment assistant. It can make mistakes and does not provide medical advice. Choose ‘Administrator’ or contact {{HUMAN_CONTACT}} to reach a person.” Disclose expected human hours in {{HUMAN_SUPPORT_HOURS}}. Promise 24-hour human availability only when actually provided.
For medical questions, complaints, uncertainty, conflicting data, deletion requests or requests for a person, stop substantive automated replies and route the enquiry through the agreed process. In emergencies the assistant must not delay seeking emergency help. Refusing AI must not block an available alternative booking method.
3 Actions and oversight
AI has no unrestricted authority. Schedule reading and appointment creation, rescheduling and cancellation require separate permissions in {{AI_ACTION_MATRIX}}. Before an action, show the patient, service without unnecessary medical detail, time and branch and obtain the scenario's required confirmation. Irreversible actions, bulk changes and exports require authorised human review. Log actions for investigation and correction.
Staff check knowledge content, prices, hours, restrictions and responses before launch and after changes. Platforma supplies agreed technical restrictions and corrects service defects. The Clinic is responsible for its information and staff decisions. AI error does not automatically remove legal or contractual liability.
4 Data and providers
Send only necessary data, using anonymised requests and internal identifiers where possible. Do not send diagnoses, scans, medical records, payment secrets or access keys. Restrict accidentally received sensitive information, apply the appropriate deletion process and do not add it to the knowledge base.
Record {{AI_PROVIDER_AND_MODEL}}, {{AI_PROCESSING_COUNTRIES}}, {{AI_RETENTION_AND_LOGGING}}, {{AI_TRAINING_SETTINGS}} and {{AI_CONTRACTUAL_SAFEGUARDS}} before activation. Missing facts must not be replaced by “data are never used for training”. Training or improving external models with client data requires its own lawful basis, agreement and necessary individual information or consent.
Document messenger and email integrations separately: provider, clinic account, fields, flow directions, countries, retention, restrictions and revocation. Check localisation, cross-border basis and medical confidentiality. An administrator connecting a channel does not establish every patient's consent.
5 Risk management and exit
Retain scenario, knowledge and model versions, test outcomes, significant errors, complaints, activation decisions and action history without excessive correspondence copies. Restrict logs and set purpose-based retention {{AI_AUDIT_RETENTION}}.
Suspend the function and route to a person on dangerous behaviour, a leak, provider-condition changes or an unverified route. Fix causes and retest before resumption. Material purpose expansion requires renewed agreement and legal assessment. External charges and limits are set in the Order; unlimited charges are not authorised.
On disconnection revoke tokens and permissions, stop dispatch queues, and return or delete data with accessible recipients under contract and law. Draft version 0.1 dated 16 October 2026. Scenario approval signatures and date: {{AI_APPROVAL_SIGNATURES_DATE}}.
